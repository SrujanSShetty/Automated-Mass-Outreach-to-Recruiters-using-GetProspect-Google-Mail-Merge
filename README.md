Automated Mass Outreach to Recruiters using GetProspect + Google Mail Merge
Description
This guide walks you through a step-by-step method to automate cold outreach to recruiters using LinkedIn data, the GetProspect Chrome Extension, and Google Mail Merge by Martin Hawksey. This is ideal for job seekers looking to scale their networking efforts with personalized mass emails.

🔧 Step-by-Step Guide
1. Install GetProspect Extension
Install the GetProspect Chrome Extension
Create your free GetProspect account
2. Collect Recruiter Emails from LinkedIn
Method 1:

Visit the “People” section of a company’s LinkedIn page
Search for keywords like "Recruiter" or "HR"
Click on the GetProspect extension and “Add to list”
Method 2:

Go to the company’s LinkedIn homepage
Open the GetProspect extension
Filter employees by job titles like "Talent Acquisition" or "Recruiter"
Add multiple recruiters to your GetProspect database in bulk
3. Export GetProspect Data
Go to your GetProspect dashboard
Export the list as a CSV file
It typically includes names, emails, company, and job titles
4. Prepare Mail Merge Google Sheet
Make a copy of Martin Hawksey’s Mail Merge Sheet
Paste recruiter email data into the sheet
Ensure columns like First Name, Email, and optionally Company are present
Personalization tags like {{First Name}} or {{Company}} must match column headers
5. Draft the Email Template in Gmail
Go to Gmail → Compose → Save this as a draft
Example Template:
Subject: Interest in Business Intelligence Data Analyst Role

Dear Recruiter,

I hope you’re doing well. I’m interested in the Business Intelligence Data Analyst role at your esteemed organization and would love to apply. I have two years of experience as a Business Intelligence Data Analyst (SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI, Tableau) and am currently pursuing my MS in Data Analytics at IIT, Chicago.

I’d love the opportunity to discuss how my skills align with this role. Please find my resume attached for your reference. Let me know if you’d be open to a quick chat.

Looking forward to your response!

Best regards,  
Srujan Shekar Shetty  
📞 312-XXX-XXXX  
📧 srujansshettyofficial@gmail.com  
🔗 LinkedIn | Portfolio
6. Run the Mail Merge Script
Back in the Google Sheet, click on “Mail Merge” > “Start Mail Merge”
Emails will be sent out automatically from your Gmail account
✅ Pro Tip
Gmail allows up to 1,500 recipients/day, so you can reach that many recruiters in just one click!

📊 Pros & Cons
Pros	Cons
✅ Reach up to 1,500 recruiters/day	❌ Some emails may be invalid (bounce-back risk)
✅ Saves time with automation	❌ Limited personalization if template isn't edited properly
✅ Scalable job outreach method	❌ Risk of being marked as spam if overused
✅ Easy to set up and repeat	❌ Gmail limits apply unless using paid workspace
